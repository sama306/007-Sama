Forza Horizon 6 takes the award-winning open-world racing series to Southeast Asia, featuring diverse biomes from tropical islands to bustling metropolises. The game introduces dynamic weather that changes based on real-world seasons, a revamped car physics engine, and the largest car roster in franchise history.

With seamless multiplayer integration, a deep campaign with hundreds of events, and the return of fan-favorite features like Horizon Stories and Eliminator, Forza Horizon 6 is the ultimate celebration of automotive culture.
