God of War Ragnarök is an action-adventure game developed by Santa Monica Studio and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. It continues the story of Kratos and his son Atreus as they navigate the impending Norse apocalypse, Ragnarök. The game features visceral combat, stunning visuals, and an emotionally charged narrative that concludes the Norse saga.
