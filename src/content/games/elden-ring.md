Elden Ring is an action role-playing game developed by FromSoftware and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. The game was directed by Hidetaka Miyazaki and features world-building by fantasy novelist George R. R. Martin.

Set in the vast and interconnected world of the Lands Between, players take on the role of a Tarnished who must traverse stunning landscapes, defeat powerful demigods, and ultimately claim the title of Elden Lord. The game features deep character customization, a wide variety of weapons and spells, and a rich multiplayer component.
