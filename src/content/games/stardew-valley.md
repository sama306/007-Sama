Stardew Valley is an open-ended farming simulation game developed by Eric Barone. Players inherit a rundown farm in the small town of Stardew Valley and must work to restore it to its former glory. The game offers a wide range of activities including farming, fishing, mining, foraging, and building relationships with the townspeople.
