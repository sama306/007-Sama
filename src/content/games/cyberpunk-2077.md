Cyberpunk 2077 is an open-world action role-playing game developed by CD Projekt Red. Set in the sprawling metropolis of Night City, players take on the role of V, a mercenary outlaw searching for a unique implant that holds the key to immortality. The game features a branching narrative, deep character customization, and a vibrant dystopian world to explore.
