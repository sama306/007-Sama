Age of Empires IV is a real-time strategy video game developed by Relic Entertainment and published by Xbox Game Studios. It is the fourth main installment in the Age of Empires series.

The game features eight civilizations at launch, including the English, French, Mongols, and Chinese, each with unique units, technologies, and playstyles. Players can engage in four campaigns spanning medieval history, including the Norman Conquest and the Hundred Years' War, or compete in multiplayer battles.
