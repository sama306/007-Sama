The Witcher IV marks the beginning of a new saga in the acclaimed RPG series. Players take on the role of Ciri, now a fully-fledged witcher, as she embarks on her own path through a war-torn continent. Developed on CD Projekt Red's Unreal Engine 5 technology, the game promises unprecedented visual fidelity and a deeply reactive world.

The story explores themes of destiny, identity, and the consequences of power, set in entirely new regions of the Witcher universe while honoring the legacy of Geralt of Rivia.
