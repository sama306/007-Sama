Sid Meier's Civilization VI is a turn-based strategy game developed by Firaxis Games. Players guide a civilization from the ancient era to the modern age, making strategic decisions about technology, culture, warfare, and diplomacy. The game features unstacked cities and active tech research, allowing players to customize their civilization's development path.
