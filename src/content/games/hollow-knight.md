Hollow Knight is a metroidvania action-adventure game developed and published by Team Cherry. Players control a nameless knight exploring the decaying kingdom of Hallownest, a vast underground world filled with terrifying creatures, hidden secrets, and challenging bosses. The game is renowned for its hand-drawn art style, atmospheric soundtrack, and tight responsive gameplay.
