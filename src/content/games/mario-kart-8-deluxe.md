Mario Kart 8 Deluxe is a kart racing game developed and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo Switch. It is an enhanced port of the Wii U original, featuring all DLC content, new characters, and improved battle modes. With 48 tracks, a roster of 42 characters, and both local and online multiplayer, it remains the definitive Mario Kart experience.
