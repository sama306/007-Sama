Tekken 8 is a fighting game developed and published by Bandai Namco Entertainment. The latest entry in the acclaimed Tekken series introduces the Heat system, a new aggressive mechanic that enables powerful offensive options. With a diverse roster of fighters, stunning graphics powered by Unreal Engine 5, and robust online features, Tekken 8 delivers an exhilarating fighting game experience.
