Fable is a reboot of the beloved action RPG franchise, developed by Playground Games, the studio behind the Forza Horizon series. Set in the whimsical and dangerous world of Albion, this new entry reimagines the series for a new generation with cutting-edge visuals and deep role-playing mechanics.

Players forge their own path through a world that reacts to their moral choices, appearance, and reputation. The game features an expansive open world filled with magical creatures, quirky characters, and ancient mysteries.
