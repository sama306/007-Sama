Grand Theft Auto VI returns to the sun-drenched streets of Vice City and the wider state of Leonida in this ambitious new entry in the blockbuster series. The story follows dual protagonists Bonnie and Clyde-inspired duo as they navigate the criminal underworld.

Built on the RAGE 9 engine, GTA VI features the most detailed open world ever created by Rockstar, with dynamic weather systems, enhanced AI, and a living, breathing city that evolves based on player actions.
