Cities: Skylines II is a city-building simulation game developed by Colossal Order and published by Paradox Interactive. It expands upon the foundation of its predecessor with more complex economic systems, improved traffic AI, detailed citizen simulation, and extensive customization options. Players can build sprawling metropolises while managing budgets, services, and citizen happiness.
