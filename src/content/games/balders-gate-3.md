Baldur's Gate 3 is a role-playing video game developed and published by Larian Studios. Based on the Dungeons & Dragons 5th edition ruleset, the game offers unprecedented player freedom and reactivity. Players create a character or choose from origin characters, assemble a party, and embark on an epic adventure filled with deep tactical combat, meaningful choices, and complex characters.
