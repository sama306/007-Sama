Hades is a rogue-like dungeon crawler developed and published by Supergiant Games. Players control Zagreus, the son of Hades, as he attempts to escape the Underworld to reach Mount Olympus.

Each escape attempt offers unique boons from the Olympian gods, each with their own distinct playstyle. With its fast-paced combat, rich narrative, and stunning hand-drawn art, Hades has won numerous Game of the Year awards and continues to be celebrated as one of the best games of its generation.
