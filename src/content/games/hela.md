Hela is a mythic action-adventure game from Arrowhead Game Studios that plunges players into the Norse underworld. Players take on the role of a fallen warrior who must battle through Helheim, forge uneasy alliances with legendary figures, and uncover the truth behind a cosmic imbalance threatening all realms.

The game features fluid hack-and-slash combat, a unique death-mechanic that transforms the player with each resurrection, and a rich world inspired by Norse mythology. Developed with a focus on tight combat and environmental storytelling.
