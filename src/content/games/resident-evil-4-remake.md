Resident Evil 4 Remake is a survival horror game developed and published by Capcom. It is a remake of the 2005 classic Resident Evil 4, following special agent Leon S. Kennedy on a mission to rescue the President's daughter from a mysterious cult in a remote European village. The game combines intense combat, resource management, and atmospheric horror with modern gameplay mechanics and stunning visuals.
