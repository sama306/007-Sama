Star Wars: Zero Company is a tactical third-person action game developed by Respawn Entertainment, the creators of Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order and Survivor. Set between Episodes III and IV, the game follows a newly formed rebel cell known as Zero Company as they execute daring missions against Imperial forces.

Players command a squad of unique operatives, each with distinct abilities and backstories, in strategic combat encounters. The game features a deep cover system, destructible environments, and a branching narrative that responds to player choices.
